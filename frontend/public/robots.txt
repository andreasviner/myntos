# Everything here may be fetched, by search engines and AI crawlers alike: we
# would rather an assistant answered a question about Myntos from our own pages
# than from a guess. There is a summary written for them at /llms.txt, with one
# help article per task under /llms/help/ and the API skills under /llms/skills/.
#
# The application pages are kept out of the index by a noindex in their own
# <head>, and out of the sitemap, both decided by frontend/src/lib/indexing.ts.
# They are deliberately not refused here as well. A crawler that may not fetch a
# page never reads its noindex, and Google then indexes the bare URL from any
# link to it; that is how this file used to work for four of them.
