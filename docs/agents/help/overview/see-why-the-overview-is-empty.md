# See why the overview is empty

The overview shows one company's year. It is empty in two different
situations: the account has no company's books yet, or the company has books
but nothing has been registered in them this year. The page says which.

## They might ask

- «Hvorfor er oversikten min tom?»
- «Det står «Ingen regnskap ennå», hva gjør jeg?»
- «Hvorfor vises ingen tall på forsiden?»
- "Why is my Myntos dashboard empty?"

## Before they start

They need to be signed in.

## Steps

1. Open `https://myntos.no/` («Oversikt» in the top menu) and read what it
   says.
2. **«Ingen regnskap ennå»**: the account has no company yet. Click «Legg til
   selskapet ditt» to set up the books: Myntos sets up the chart of accounts,
   VAT codes and the financial year by itself, and fetches the company from
   Altinn if the person has rights there.
3. **«Du har ikke tilgang til å opprette selskaper i denne organisasjonen. Be
   en administrator om å gjøre det.»** under that card: they may not add
   companies. An administrator adds the company, or invites them to one that
   exists.
4. **«Ingen betalinger registrert i Myntos ennå.»** under «Siste bevegelser»,
   and «Ingen tall å vise for i år.» under «Største poster i år»: no payment
   has been registered in Myntos this year. Click «Registrer betaling» to
   register the first one. Vouchers brought over from another program, the
   opening balance and manual vouchers are never listed there: «alle bilag» in
   the same line, or «Se alle bilag →», opens «Bilag», where they are:
   [See every voucher in the books](https://myntos.no/llms/help/vouchers/see-every-voucher.md).
   While the books are empty, the card «Kommer du fra et annet
   regnskapsprogram?» with «Hent regnskapet» leads to the import from the old
   program's SAF-T files; while an import is being read or waits to be checked,
   the same card says so («Myntos leser SAF-T-filene», «SAF-T-filene er lest»).
5. **«Vi fikk ikke hentet oversikten.»**, or another message at the top:
   loading failed. Reload the page.

## Good to know

- Someone invited to a company's books sees that company here once they have
  accepted the invitation.
- The figures are for the current calendar year. A company whose payments are
  all from earlier years shows nothing under «Største poster i år», but its
  older payments are still under «Betaling».
