# Switch to another company

One Myntos account can have access to several companies' books: an owner with
two businesses, or an accountant (regnskapsfører) with many clients. Every
page works on one company at a time, the one named at the top right of the
screen. Switching changes it for every page, and the page reloads so nothing
from the previous company stays on screen.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan bytter jeg selskap i Myntos?»
- «Hvordan ser jeg regnskapet til en annen klient?»
- «Hvorfor ser jeg feil selskap?»
- "How do I switch between companies in Myntos?"

## Before they start

They need access to the other company. A company they have not been invited
to, or added themselves, is not in the list.

## Steps

1. On the overview (`https://myntos.no/`), click «Bytt selskap» next to the
   organisasjonsnummer under the company's name. On any other page, click the
   company's name at the top right of the screen instead (on a phone, open the
   menu first).
2. The menu «Selskaper» lists every company they have access to,
   alphabetically, each with its organisasjonsnummer. The one showing now has
   a tick.
3. Click the company to switch to. The page reloads with that company's name
   at the top and its figures.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No «Bytt selskap» on the overview | They have access to only one company | Nothing to switch to. To get access to another, someone there must invite them, or they add the company with «Legg til selskap» |
| The company is not in the list | They have no access to it | Ask someone in that company to invite them |
| A small dot next to a company's name | Nobody has confirmed in Altinn that they may act for it yet; the books work as before | An administrator confirms it on the company page |

## Good to know

- The choice is remembered in the browser, so Myntos opens on the same
  company next time.
- The same menu has «Legg til selskap» (to set up books for another company,
  shown to those allowed to), «Innstillinger for selskapet» and «Din konto».
- A role belongs to one company: the person may be «Eier» in their own books
  and «Leser» in a client's, and each page follows the role in the company
  showing.
- Everything a person does applies to the company showing. Check the name at
  the top before registering anything.
