# Delete a customer or supplier

Customers and suppliers cannot be deleted in Myntos, by anyone. Entries in
the books and the SAF-T exports refer to them by number, and removing one
would either break those references or rewrite history. One that was never
used does no harm by staying in the list.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan sletter jeg en kunde?»
- «Jeg har lagt inn samme leverandør to ganger»
- «Jeg registrerte en kunde som leverandør ved en feil»
- "How do I delete a customer in Myntos?"

## Before they start

- Nothing; there is no delete button for anyone.

## Steps

1. A duplicate: use one of them from now on, and leave the other unused.
   Renaming the unused one makes the choice easier to see, for instance by
   adding «(ikke bruk)» with «Endre navn»:
   [Rename a customer or supplier](https://myntos.no/llms/help/customers-and-suppliers/rename-a-customer-or-supplier.md).
2. The wrong type (a customer registered as a supplier, or the other way
   round): the type cannot be changed. Register it again with the right type:
   [Add a customer](https://myntos.no/llms/help/customers-and-suppliers/add-a-customer.md)
   or [add a supplier](https://myntos.no/llms/help/customers-and-suppliers/add-a-supplier.md).

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No delete button | Deleting is not possible | Follow the steps above |

## Good to know

- A business that both buys from and sells to the company is registered
  twice, once as each type, with two numbers.
