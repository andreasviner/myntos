# See the documents for a payment

Every file attached to a payment is kept in the bilag archive and can be
opened from the register on the «Betaling» page. The files are kept as they
were uploaded, and none can be removed: the law requires them to be kept for
five years after the financial year ends.

## They might ask

- «Hvor finner jeg kvitteringene jeg har lastet opp?»
- «Hvordan laster jeg ned et bilag fra Myntos?»
- «Hvilke filer hører til denne betalingen?»
- "Where are my receipts stored in Myntos?"

## Before they start

Anyone with access to the company can see the documents, whatever their role.

## Steps

1. Open «Betaling» in the top menu (`https://myntos.no/kontantstrom/`).
2. Find the payment in the register below the form. The «Bilagsfil» column
   shows:
   - one file: a badge with its kind and size, «PDF 184 kB» or «BILDE 2.1 MB».
     Click it to download the file.
   - several files: «3 FILER». Click it to open the list.
   - nothing, or «Legg ved bilag»: no file is attached yet.
3. For the full list, click «Vis info» at the end of the row. Under the
   payment's details, «Bilagsfil» or «Bilagsfiler (3)» lists every file by
   name, with a small picture of each photo. Click a file to download it.
   «Skjul info» closes it again.

## Good to know

- Files are downloaded rather than opened in the page; the browser saves
  them or opens them in its own viewer.
- A payment that was corrected shows the files of every entry in the
  correction together, because the receipt stays on the original entry.
- The register on the page shows the 200 most recent entries. Older payments
  and their files are still in the books and the archive, but are not listed
  on the page.
