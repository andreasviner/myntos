# Find the VAT deadline

Every termin has one deadline, for sending the MVA-melding and for paying
what it comes to. Myntos shows it beside each termin, worked out from the
termin length the company has.

## They might ask

- «Når er fristen for mva-meldingen?»
- «Når må mvaen være betalt?»
- «Hva er fristen for 3. termin?»
- "When is the VAT return due in Norway?"

## Before they start

Any role can read the page. The termin length on the page («Terminlengde»)
must match what Skatteetaten has given the company, or the deadlines will be
for the wrong periods:
[Change the VAT period or category](https://myntos.no/llms/help/vat/change-the-vat-period-or-category.md).

## Steps

1. Open `https://myntos.no/mva/` (from «Oversikt», the card
   «Merverdiavgift», «Mva-meldingen →»).
2. The column «Frist» in the table is each termin's deadline.
3. «Se meldingen» on a termin shows it again in the card: «Frist for melding
   og betaling …».

## Good to know

- Two months («Annenhver måned (vanlig)»): one month and ten days after the
  termin ends, so 1. termin (January–February) is due 10 April. The
  exception is 3. termin (May–June), due 31 August.
- One month («Månedlig»): also one month and ten days after; January is due
  10 March.
- A year («Årlig»): 10 March the year after, or 10 April for «Primærnæring
  (jord, skog, fiske)».
- A deadline on a Saturday or Sunday moves to the Monday. Public holidays are
  not taken into account, so the date Myntos shows can be a day early, never
  late.
- The melding can be sent from the day after the termin ends.
- A termin with nothing in it is still due, with zero.
