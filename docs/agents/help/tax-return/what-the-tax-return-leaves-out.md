# What the tax return from Myntos leaves out

Myntos builds what the books can say and asks the one thing they cannot.
Some parts of a tax return are neither, and Myntos says so rather than guess.
The page lists each one that applies as a merknad under «Inntektsåret …».

## They might ask

- «Kan jeg sende skattemeldingen for enkeltpersonforetaket fra Myntos?»
- «Hva må jeg fylle ut selv på skatteetaten.no?»
- «Hvorfor er ikke eiendommen med i formuen?»
- "Can Myntos file the tax return for a sole proprietorship?"

## What is left out

- **An enkeltpersonforetak (ENK).** Its næringsspesifikasjon goes with the
  owner's own skattemelding, on the owner's fødselsnummer, and Skatteetaten
  builds several of its posts from specifications Myntos does not send yet.
  The page says «Myntos lager ennå ikke næringsspesifikasjonen for et
  enkeltpersonforetak. …». The owner fills it in on skatteetaten.no, with the
  figures from «Regnskap»
  ([See the accounts for every year](https://myntos.no/llms/help/reports/see-the-accounts-for-every-year.md)).
- **Real estate and shares in the formue.** Their value for formue is set by
  Skatteetaten's own rules, not by the books. Add them on skatteetaten.no
  after sending, under the skattemelding's real estate and shares.
- **Time-limited rights and assets that are not depreciated.** Myntos sends
  saldo groups a to j only, and the page says so for any other asset.
- **The skattemelding's other questions** (konsernbidrag, research projects,
  restructuring and the like): Myntos leaves Skatteetaten's draft as it is,
  and the person completes it on skatteetaten.no after sending, if it applies.

## Good to know

- Any other organisation form than AS and ENK (ANS, DA, SA, …) is not filed
  from Myntos.
