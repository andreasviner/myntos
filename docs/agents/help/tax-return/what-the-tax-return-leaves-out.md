# What the tax return from Myntos leaves out

Myntos builds the næringsspesifikasjon and skattemelding for both
**aksjeselskap (AS)** and **enkeltpersonforetak (ENK)** from the closed year's
books. Some parts of a tax return do not live in the books, and Myntos leaves
them alone rather than guessing. The page lists each one that applies as a
merknad under «Inntektsåret …».

## They might ask

- «Kan jeg sende skattemeldingen for enkeltpersonforetaket fra Myntos?»
- «Hva må jeg fylle ut selv på skatteetaten.no?»
- «Hvorfor er ikke eiendommen med i formuen?»
- "Can Myntos file the tax return for a sole proprietorship?"

## What is left out

- **Personal items on an ENK tax return.** Myntos sends the ENK's
  næringsspesifikasjon together with the owner's personal skattemelding on
  their fødselsnummer, keeping every personal post (salary, private bank
  accounts, mortgages, deductions) untouched. By default Myntos sends with
  «Send inn næringsdelen og fullfør på skatteetaten.no (anbefalt)», so the
  owner opens skatteetaten.no afterwards to review personal items and press
  «Lever».
- **Real estate and shares in the formue.** Their value for formue is set by
  Skatteetaten's own rules, not by the books. Add them on skatteetaten.no
  after sending, under the skattemelding's real estate and shares.
- **Time-limited rights and assets that are not depreciated.** Myntos sends
  saldo groups a to j only, and the page says so for any other asset.
- **Special farming tax cards (jordbrukskonto and tømmerkonto).** Livestock
  and stock counts from `/buskap/` are included in the ENK varelager
  specification, while jordbrukskonto (§ 14-82) and skog- og tømmerkonto
  (§ 14-81) are added on skatteetaten.no if the farm uses them.
- **The skattemelding's other special cards** (konsernbidrag, SkatteFUNN,
  restructuring and the like): Myntos leaves Skatteetaten's draft as it is,
  and the person completes it on skatteetaten.no after sending, if it applies.

## Good to know

- Any other organisation form than AS and ENK (ANS, DA, SA, …) is not filed
  from Myntos.
