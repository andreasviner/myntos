# Follow up on what you reported

Feedback to Myntos is anonymous, so the link the person got when they sent it
is the only way back to it. With it they see the status and Myntos's reply,
and can withdraw it. Without it nothing can be done: Myntos does not know who
sent what.

## They might ask

- «Har Myntos svart på tilbakemeldingen min?»
- «Hvordan sletter jeg det jeg sendte til Myntos?»
- «Jeg skrev noe jeg ikke skulle i tilbakemeldingen»
- "Did Myntos reply to my bug report?"

## Before they start

The link from when they sent it, `https://myntos.no/tilbakemelding/#ref=…`.
If they have lost it, see the table below.

## Steps

1. Open the link. The card «Din tilbakemelding» shows «Type», «Sendt» and
   «Status», and the text they sent.
2. The status is one of «Ikke lest ennå», «Lest», «Planlagt», «Ferdig» or
   «Blir ikke gjort».
3. If Myntos has answered, the answer is under «Svar fra Myntos».
4. To take it back, press «Trekk tilbake» and confirm. **It is deleted and
   cannot be brought back**, together with anything Myntos published about it.
   The page says «Trukket tilbake. Den er slettet hos oss.»

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| «Finner ingen tilbakemelding med den referansen. …» | Withdrawn, deleted a year after it was closed, or the link is incomplete | Check that the whole link was copied. Otherwise send it again if it still matters |
| They have lost the link | Nothing ties the report to them | Nothing to recover. Send it again; to have something removed, write to kontakt@myntos.no describing it, and Myntos can delete it once found |

## Good to know

- Myntos reads feedback by hand. A status that stays «Ikke lest ennå» for a
  few days is normal.
- Done and declined reports are deleted a year after they were closed.
