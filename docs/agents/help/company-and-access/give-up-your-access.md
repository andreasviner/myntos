# Give up your access

Anyone can walk away from a company's books, whatever their role: an
accountant whose client has moved on, or a colleague who no longer needs
access. The books themselves are not touched, and the person's own account
and other companies stay as they are. Getting back in takes a new
invitation.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan fjerner jeg et selskap fra listen min i Myntos?»
- «Jeg er ikke lenger regnskapsfører for denne kunden. Hvordan går jeg ut?»
- "How do I leave a company in Myntos?"

## Before they start

- Any role. The last «Eier» must first make someone else «Eier»:
  [Change someone's role](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/change-someones-role.md).
- Make sure the right company is showing at the top right; this gives up
  access to that one.

## Steps

1. Open the company page: click the company's name at the top right and
   choose «Innstillinger for selskapet» (`https://myntos.no/organisasjon/`).
2. Under «Tilgang», in the table «Medlemmer i …», find their own row, marked
   «(deg)».
3. Press «Gi fra deg tilgangen».
4. The browser asks «Gi fra deg tilgangen til dette regnskapet? Du kommer ikke
   inn i det igjen uten en ny invitasjon.» Press OK. They are taken to the
   overview, and the company is gone from their company menu.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| «the organization would be left without an owner; appoint another owner first» | They are the last «Eier» | Make someone else «Eier» first |
| «Medlemmet kunne ikke fjernes.» | It failed for another reason | Reload the page and try again |

## Good to know

- This cannot be undone by the person themselves: someone still in the books
  has to invite them again.
- Every AI key they made for these books stops for good.
- This is not deleting the account. Deleting the whole Myntos account is done
  under «Din konto».
