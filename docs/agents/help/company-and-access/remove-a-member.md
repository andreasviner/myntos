# Remove a member

Removing someone takes away their access to this company's books at once.
What they registered stays in the books, with their name on it, because the
books must show who made each entry. Their access to other companies is not
affected.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan fjerner jeg en bruker fra regnskapet?»
- «Regnskapsføreren vår har sluttet. Hvordan tar jeg bort tilgangen?»
- "How do I remove someone's access in Myntos?"

## Before they start

- The role «Administrator» or «Eier» in this company. Only an «Eier» can
  remove another «Eier».

## Steps

1. Open the company page: click the company's name at the top right and
   choose «Innstillinger for selskapet» (`https://myntos.no/organisasjon/`).
2. Under «Tilgang», in the table «Medlemmer i …», find the person.
3. Press «Fjern» at the end of their row.
4. The browser asks «Fjerne … sin tilgang til dette regnskapet?» Press OK.
   They disappear from the list.

## If something stops them

The message appears at the top of the page.

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No «Fjern» on other people's rows | Their role is «Leser» or «Medlem» | Ask an «Administrator» or «Eier» |
| «only an owner can modify another owner» | An «Administrator» tried to remove an «Eier» | Ask an «Eier» |
| «the organization would be left without an owner; appoint another owner first» | It is the last «Eier» | Make someone else «Eier» first: [Change someone's role](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/change-someones-role.md) |
| «Medlemmet kunne ikke fjernes.» | The removal failed for another reason | Reload the page and try again |

## Good to know

- Every AI key the removed person made for these books stops for good at the
  same time. Inviting them back later does not wake those keys.
- To let them back in, send a new invitation:
  [Invite a colleague](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/invite-a-colleague.md).
- A pending invitation is not a member; it is withdrawn instead:
  [Withdraw an invitation](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/withdraw-an-invitation.md).
- No AI agent with a key can remove anyone.
