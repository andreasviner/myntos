# Invite a board member

The board list on the company page marks people who cannot open the books
with «Ingen tilgang», and offers «Inviter» right there. Inviting from the row
works like an ordinary invitation, but the person's name from the register
goes with it, so the row says «Invitert» until they accept.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan gir jeg styrelederen tilgang til regnskapet?»
- «Daglig leder står med "Ingen tilgang". Hva gjør jeg?»
- "How do I invite a board member in Myntos?"

## Before they start

- The role «Administrator» or «Eier» in this company.
- The board member's e-mail address. The register does not have it.
- The register has been fetched, so the «Roller» table is there:
  [Fetch the company from Enhetsregisteret again](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/fetch-from-enhetsregisteret-again.md).

## Steps

1. Open the company page: click the company's name at the top right and
   choose «Innstillinger for selskapet» (`https://myntos.no/organisasjon/`).
2. Scroll to «Fra Enhetsregisteret», table «Roller», and find the person. The
   «Merknad» column says «Ingen tilgang».
3. Press «Inviter» under the mark. The invitation form opens under the member
   list, saying «Invitasjon til …, fra styret i Enhetsregisteret.»
4. Type their «E-postadresse» and choose a «Rolle».
5. Press «Send invitasjon». The person's row in «Roller» now says «Invitert»,
   with the address underneath.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No «Inviter» on the row | Their role is «Leser» or «Medlem», or the row says something other than «Ingen tilgang» | Ask an «Administrator» or «Eier». A row with «Har trolig tilgang» already has a member by that name |
| Messages after «Send invitasjon» | The same as for any invitation | [Invite a colleague](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/invite-a-colleague.md) |

## Good to know

- The name is a label, not a check: whoever accepts must sign in with the
  invited address, whatever their name.
- When the person accepts, the mark becomes «Har trolig tilgang» if their
  Myntos account carries the same name, and «Har tilgang» once they link
  ID-porten:
  [Link your ID-porten login](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/link-your-id-porten-login.md).
