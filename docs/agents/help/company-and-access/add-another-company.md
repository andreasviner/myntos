# Add another company

One Myntos account can keep books for as many companies as it needs, and
each company is a separate set of books with its own subscription, its own
members and its own settings. Setting one up takes a minute: Myntos fetches
the company from Altinn or Enhetsregisteret, so little has to be typed. The
person who sets it up is the only one with access until they invite others.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan legger jeg til et selskap til i Myntos?»
- «Jeg har fått en ny klient. Hvordan setter jeg opp regnskapet?»
- «Hvor er "Legg til selskap"?»
- "How do I add a second company in Myntos?"

## Before they start

- An account of their own that may set up books. Access given by invitation
  to someone else's books does not let them add companies there, and then
  «Legg til selskap» is not shown.
- The company's organisation number, or a login with ID-porten so Myntos can
  list the companies they have rights for in Altinn.

## Steps

1. Click the company's name at the top right of any page.
2. At the bottom of the menu, press «Legg til selskap». It opens the page
   «Sett opp regnskapet» (`https://myntos.no/nytt-selskap/`).
3. Choose the company from Altinn («Hent selskapene mine»), or type its
   organisation number under «Skriv inn organisasjonsnummeret» and press
   «Neste».
4. Answer the questions under «Litt om regnskapet» and press «Sett opp
   regnskapet».
5. When it says «Regnskapet er satt opp», invite colleagues if they need
   access, and press «Åpne regnskapet».

A person with no books at all sees «Ingen regnskap ennå» on the company page
instead, with «Sett opp regnskapet», which goes to the same place.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No «Legg til selskap» in the menu | Their access is only by invitation, or their role on their account does not allow setting up books | Set up books from their own account, or ask the account's owner to do it |
| The company is already in their menu | Books for it exist on their account already | Switch to it instead: [Switch between companies](https://myntos.no/llms/help/overview/switch-company.md) |

## Good to know

- The new company starts unconfirmed:
  [Confirm the company with ID-porten](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/confirm-the-company.md).
- Add its bank account before the first invoice:
  [Add a bank account](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/add-a-bank-account.md).
- Nobody else on the account gets access automatically, not even colleagues
  in the other companies:
  [Invite a colleague](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/invite-a-colleague.md).
- An AI agent with a key cannot set up companies.
