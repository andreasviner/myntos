# Correct a bank account number

A bank account on the company page can be corrected in place: the number, its
name, or both. The correction applies to invoices made from now on. Invoices
already issued keep the number they were issued with, and an issued invoice
can only be corrected with a credit note (kreditnota).

## They might ask

- «Kontonummeret på fakturaen er feil, hvordan retter jeg det?»
- «Hvordan endrer jeg navnet på en bankkonto i Myntos?»
- "How do I fix a wrong account number in Myntos?"

## Before they start

- The role «Administrator» or «Eier» in this company.

## Steps

1. Open the company page: click the company's name at the top right and
   choose «Innstillinger for selskapet» (`https://myntos.no/organisasjon/`).
2. In «Selskapet», under «Kontonummer», press the pencil at the end of the
   account's line («Endre kontonummeret»).
3. Correct the number in the first box, or the name in the second.
4. Press «Lagre». The list says «Lagret.» «Avbryt» leaves it as it was.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No pencil | Their role is «Leser» or «Medlem» | Ask an «Administrator» or «Eier» |
| «kontonummeret … har feil kontrollsiffer. Kontroller det mot nettbanken» | A typo in the number | Copy it from the online bank again |
| «selskapet har allerede kontonummer …» | The new number is already another line on the list | Remove one of the two: [Remove a bank account](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/remove-a-bank-account.md) |

## Good to know

- An invoice that went out with the wrong number cannot be edited. Credit it
  and issue a new one, or tell the customer the right account.
- The name is only for telling accounts apart; it is not printed on invoices.
- No AI agent with a Myntos key can change an account number. If someone asks
  for a change by e-mail or message, check with them by phone first: changed
  account numbers are a common fraud.
