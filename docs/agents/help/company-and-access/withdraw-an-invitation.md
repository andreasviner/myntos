# Withdraw an invitation

A pending invitation can be withdrawn until it is accepted. The link in the
e-mail stops working at once. This is also how to fix an invitation with the
wrong address or the wrong role: withdraw it and send a new one.

## They might ask

- «Jeg sendte invitasjonen til feil adresse»
- «Hvordan avbryter jeg en invitasjon?»
- "How do I cancel an invitation in Myntos?"

## Before they start

- The role «Administrator» or «Eier» in this company.

## Steps

1. Open the company page: click the company's name at the top right and
   choose «Innstillinger for selskapet» (`https://myntos.no/organisasjon/`).
2. Under «Tilgang», find the card «Ventende invitasjoner».
3. Press «Trekk tilbake» on the invitation's row. There is no confirmation;
   it disappears from the list.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No «Ventende invitasjoner» card | No pending invitations, or their role is «Leser» or «Medlem» | If the person already accepted, they are a member: [Remove a member](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/remove-a-member.md) |
| «Invitasjonen kunne ikke trekkes tilbake.» or «invitasjonen finnes ikke» | It was accepted or withdrawn in the meantime | Reload the page |

## Good to know

- Withdrawing an invitation sent from the board list puts that row back to
  «Ingen tilgang».
- Nobody is told by e-mail that an invitation was withdrawn.
