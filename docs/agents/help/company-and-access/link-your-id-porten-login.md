# Link your ID-porten login

The member list on the company page has a column «ID-porten». It says whether
each person has proved who they are by logging in with ID-porten (BankID or
similar). Myntos then knows the person's name as Folkeregisteret has it, which
is the same source Enhetsregisteret's board list uses. That is what lets the
board list say «Har tilgang» about a board member instead of only «Har trolig
tilgang».

## They might ask

- «Hva betyr "Koble til ID-porten" i medlemslisten?»
- «Hvorfor står det "Har trolig tilgang" ved navnet mitt i styret?»
- «Hva betyr "Ikke koblet"?»
- "Why does Myntos say I probably have access?"

## Before they start

- Any role can do it, for themselves only. Nobody can link someone else.
- BankID, Buypass or Commfides. MinID is not enough.

## Steps

1. Open the company page: click the company's name at the top right and
   choose «Innstillinger for selskapet» (`https://myntos.no/organisasjon/`).
2. Under «Tilgang», in the table «Medlemmer i …», find their own row, marked
   «(deg)».
3. In the «ID-porten» column, press «Koble til ID-porten» («Da vet vi hvem du
   er i styret.»).
4. Log in with ID-porten. They come back to the same page, and the column
   says «Bekreftet».

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| «Innloggingen ga ikke høyt nok sikkerhetsnivå. Logg inn med BankID, Buypass eller Commfides i stedet for MinID.» | They used MinID | Try again with BankID |
| «Innloggingen ble avbrutt.» | The login was cancelled | Try again |
| «Ikke koblet» on a colleague's row, with no button | Only the colleague can link themselves | Ask them to do it from their own login |

## Good to know

- The column shows «Bekreftet», and the name ID-porten gave when it differs
  from the account's name. The fødselsnummer is never shown to anyone.
- Logging in to Myntos itself with BankID does not count. The link is made
  only through ID-porten.
- Confirming the company with ID-porten links the person's login in the same
  step.
- Linking gives no extra access. It only makes the board list more certain.
