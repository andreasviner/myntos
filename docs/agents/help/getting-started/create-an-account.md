# Create a Myntos account

A Myntos account belongs to one person and is free. It holds no books by
itself: once it exists, the person either sets up the books for their own
company or accepts an invitation to somebody else's. Signing up takes a name,
an e-mail address and a password, or one press on Google or Microsoft when the
page offers them. The one thing worth getting right is the name: it is what
the books show beside every entry the person registers.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan oppretter jeg en konto i Myntos?»
- «Hvordan registrerer jeg meg?»
- «Kan jeg registrere meg med Google?»
- "How do I sign up for Myntos?"

## Before they start

- **Invited by somebody?** Start from the link in the invitation e-mail
  instead. It fills in the address the invitation was sent to and brings them
  back to accept it afterwards. See
  [Accept an invitation](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/accept-an-invitation.md).
- **Want to sign in with BankID?** That is a different path with a
  consequence: an account made with BankID signs in only with BankID. See
  [Sign up with BankID](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/sign-up-with-bankid.md).

## Steps

1. Press «Opprett konto» at the top right of myntos.no
   (`https://myntos.no/registrer/`). The page is headed «Opprett konto».
2. **With Google or Microsoft:** press «Registrer med Google» or «Registrer
   med Microsoft» and sign in there. These buttons appear only when this
   Myntos offers them. The account is created with the name and address the
   provider gives, and they come back signed in. Skip the rest of the steps.
3. **With e-mail:** fill in the form under «eller med e-post»:
   - «Navn»: their own name, as colleagues and the books should show it.
   - «E-postadresse»: an address they read. Confirmation links, password
     resets and invitations go there.
   - «Passord»: the hint under the field gives the minimum length (it reads
     «Minst 10 tegn» on myntos.no). «Lengde teller mer enn krøllete tegn»: a
     long passphrase beats a short, complicated password.
   - «Navn på organisasjonen (valgfritt)»: they can leave it empty (see Good
     to know).
4. Press «Opprett konto».

They are signed in at once and land on the front page. It says «Ingen
regnskap ennå» with the button «Legg til selskapet ditt», which is the next
step: [Set up the books for your company](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/set-up-the-books-for-your-company.md).

If this Myntos sends e-mail, a confirmation link goes to the address as well.
The account works fully without it; see
[Confirm your e-mail address](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/confirm-your-email-address.md).

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| «passordet må være minst 10 tegn» | The password is too short. | Choose a longer one. Nothing was created. |
| Nothing but an e-mail address in the red line, such as «ola@firma.no» | That address already has a Myntos account. The page shows the address and no explanation. | Sign in instead ([Sign in](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/sign-in.md)), or use «Glemt passordet?» if they do not remember the password. |
| An English message such as «value is not a valid email address: …» or «String should have at least 1 character» | The address is mistyped, or «Navn» is empty. These come straight from the server and are not translated. | Correct the field and press «Opprett konto» again. |
| «Det finnes allerede en Myntos-konto med denne e-postadressen, og leverandøren har ikke bekreftet at adressen er din. Logg inn på vanlig måte i stedet.» | They pressed Google or Microsoft, the address already has an account, and the provider did not vouch for the address. | Sign in with e-mail and password, then link the provider under «Din konto» ([Link Google or Microsoft](https://myntos.no/llms/help/your-account/link-google-or-microsoft.md)). |
| «Kontoen med denne e-postadressen logger inn med BankID. Logg inn med BankID i stedet.» | The address belongs to an account that signs in with BankID only. | Sign in with «Logg inn med BankID». |
| «Vi fikk ikke hentet innloggingsmetodene. Passord virker fortsatt.» | The Google, Microsoft and BankID buttons could not be loaded. | Use the e-mail form, or reload the page later. |
| «Innloggingen ble avbrutt.» | They cancelled at Google or Microsoft. | Try again, or use the e-mail form. |

## Good to know

- Creating an account accepts the «bruksvilkårene» and the
  «personvernerklæringen», linked under the form.
- **The account is the person, not the company.** Access to books is given
  one company at a time: the person who sets up a company's books owns them,
  and colleagues are invited into that company's books, not into the account.
  The page's own intro line («Kollegaer inviteres inn i den samme») is older
  than that rule.
- **The organization name does not matter.** Signing up also creates an
  account record that owns the companies the person sets up. Myntos never
  shows its name anywhere, so leaving the field empty is fine. The hint «Du
  kan endre dette senere» points at a setting no page offers today.
- **Google or Microsoft accounts have no password.** They can set one later
  with «Glemt passordet?»; see
  [Change or set your password](https://myntos.no/llms/help/your-account/change-or-set-your-password.md).
- Pressing «Logg inn med Google» or «Logg inn med Microsoft» on the sign-in
  page with an address Myntos has never seen also creates an account.
- The password is the person's alone. If they want an AI to work in the books
  for them, they make it a key of its own instead; see
  [Invite an AI](https://myntos.no/llms/help/ai/invite-an-ai.md).
