# Move from PowerOffice Go to Myntos

PowerOffice Go runs the export as a job: order it, and fetch it when it is
done. In return it lets the person choose the SAF-T version. The bilag PDFs
are a separate export, and they are what an auditor or Skatteetaten asks to
see; the SAF-T file is the figures, not the receipts.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan flytter jeg fra PowerOffice Go til Myntos?»
- «Skal jeg velge SAF-T v1.30 eller v1.40?»
- "How do I export SAF-T from PowerOffice Go?"

## Before they start

Read the general order first:
[Move your books to Myntos](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/move-your-books-to-myntos.md).
The guide is at `https://myntos.no/kom-i-gang/poweroffice-go/`, headed
«Flytte fra PowerOffice Go til Myntos».

## Steps

In PowerOffice Go, as the guide «Hent dataene ut av PowerOffice Go»
describes:

1. **«Åpne eksportlisten».** «Innstillinger → Regnskap → Eksport».
2. **«Bestill en ny eksport».** Choose «Ny eksport», set the type to «SAF-T
   Regnskap», fill in the description (it is required) and the period.
   Choose v1.30 for periods up to and including 2026; v1.40 applies from
   1 January 2027.
3. **«Hent filen når jobben er fullført».** Download the ZIP from the link in
   the message box, or from the status «Fullført» in the export list.
4. **«Eksporter bilags-PDF-ene».** The bilag documents are their own export.

Then in Myntos: create the account, set up the books, enter the opening
balance and register payments from the changeover date, as in the general
article.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The export job fails without saying why | The export needs a contact person set in the client's company settings. | Set the contact person in PowerOffice Go and order the export again. |
| They have no access to exports | As a client of an accountant, the accountant holds the access. | Ask the accountant for the files in writing; the data is the company's. |
| The menu is not where the guide says | Moved since the guide was checked on 13 August 2026. | Use the guide's «Kilder», PowerOffice's own help pages. |

## Good to know

- **What to keep** (the guide's «Ta vare på dette»): SAF-T Regnskap (ZIP) for
  each period kept; the export of bilag PDFs; payslips and payroll reports,
  if payroll was run in Go.
- **Myntos does not import the SAF-T file.** The opening balance is entered
  on «Inngående balanse»:
  [Enter an opening balance](https://myntos.no/llms/help/opening-balance-and-year-end/enter-an-opening-balance.md).
