# Move your books to Myntos from another program

Every Norwegian accounting program must be able to export the books as a
SAF-T file. Moving to Myntos is therefore mostly about getting the data
**out** of the old program safely, and starting in Myntos at a clean break
between periods. **Myntos cannot read a SAF-T file into the books today.**
The history stays in the files from the old program, which the company must
keep for five years, and the books in Myntos start from an opening balance.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan bytter jeg fra Fiken til Myntos?»
- «Kan Myntos importere SAF-T?»
- «Når er det lurt å bytte regnskapsprogram?»
- "How do I move my accounting to Myntos?"

## Before they start

Pick the guide for the program they are leaving, on «Kom i gang»
(`https://myntos.no/kom-i-gang/`, headed «Ta med deg regnskapet fra der du er
nå»), under «Velg programmet du kommer fra»:

- «Flytte fra Fiken»: [Move from Fiken](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/move-from-fiken.md)
- «Flytte fra Tripletex»: [Move from Tripletex](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/move-from-tripletex.md)
- «Flytte fra PowerOffice Go»: [Move from PowerOffice Go](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/move-from-poweroffice-go.md)
- «Flytte fra Conta»: [Move from Conta](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/move-from-conta.md)
- «Flytte fra et annet program» (DNB Regnskap, SpareBank 1, Visma, Luca and
  the rest): [Move from another program](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/move-from-another-program.md)

The «Kom i gang» menu at the top of myntos.no (shown when signed out) lists
the same guides.

## Steps

The page's checklist «Før du flytter», in its order. The order matters more
than the speed:

1. **«Flytt ved et periodeskille».** A new year is best; the start of a
   quarter works. Moving in the middle of a VAT period means taking figures
   from two systems to file one VAT return.
2. **«Fullfør lønnsåret i det gamle programmet».** If they have run payroll
   this year, finish it and send the a-meldinger from the old program. The
   a-melding is cumulative through the year.
3. **«Avslutt perioden før du eksporterer».** A SAF-T file is a snapshot.
   Anything posted in the period after the export means exporting again.
4. **«Last ned alt før du sier opp».** The duty to keep the books (as a rule
   five years after the end of the financial year) follows the company, not
   the subscription. Download before cancelling.
5. **«Lagre kopien et sted du finner den igjen».** A cloud folder with
   two-factor sign-in is enough; a laptop's downloads folder is not.

Then, under «Slik starter du i Myntos»:

6. **«Opprett konto og selskap».**
   [Create a Myntos account](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/create-an-account.md),
   then [set up the books](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/set-up-the-books-for-your-company.md)
   with «Første regnskapsår i Myntos» set to the first year they will post in
   Myntos.
7. **«Legg inn kunder og leverandører».** A customer list from the old
   program belongs here. Do this before the opening balance: unpaid invoices
   go into it one line per invoice, with the customer or supplier chosen.
8. **Enter the opening balance** from the old program's balance at the
   changeover, ideally its saldobalanse (this step is not in the page's
   list, which is older than the opening balance page):
   [Enter an opening balance](https://myntos.no/llms/help/opening-balance-and-year-end/enter-an-opening-balance.md).
9. **«Før fra og med datoen du flyttet».** Register payments in Myntos from
   the changeover date. Do not post the history again; it is in the SAF-T
   file and archive from the old program.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A menu in the old program that does not match the guide | Vendors move menus. The guides were checked against the vendors' help pages on 13 August 2026. | Each guide lists its sources under «Kilder»; the vendor's help page is newer than the guide. Searching the vendor's help for «SAF-T» is fastest. |
| The page's notice «Automatisk import er ikke bygget ennå», which also says the opening balance cannot be entered on screen | Reading SAF-T into the books is indeed not built. The part about the opening balance is older than the «Inngående balanse» page, which now exists and can read a saldobalanse as CSV. | Keep the SAF-T files as the record, and enter the opening balance on «Inngående balanse». |
| No export at all in the old program | Desktop programs and spreadsheets have no SAF-T. | The saldobalanse for the last closed year and the folder of bilag are what they need. |

## Good to know

- **Keep both the SAF-T file and the documents.** SAF-T holds the entries,
  not necessarily the receipts and invoices behind them, and bokføringsloven
  requires both.
- **Payroll, timesheets and projects are outside SAF-T Regnskap.** Export
  what they need from those parts separately.
- **SAF-T versions:** v1.30 for periods up to and including 2026, v1.40 for
  periods from 1 January 2027, if the old program asks.
