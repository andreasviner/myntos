# Confirm your e-mail address

After signing up with e-mail and password (or BankID), Myntos mails a link
that shows the address really is theirs. Opening it is all there is to do.
Confirming is not required: an unconfirmed account can do everything a
confirmed one can. The one difference is that invitations the person sends do
not carry their address as the reply address until it is confirmed.

## They might ask

- «Hva er "Bekreft e-postadressen din" på forsiden?»
- «Lenken for å bekrefte e-post virker ikke»
- «Jeg fikk aldri bekreftelses-e-posten»
- "How do I verify my email in Myntos?"

## Steps

1. Open the e-mail from Myntos and press the link. It opens
   `https://myntos.no/bekreft-epost/`, which says «Bekrefter adressen …».
2. Nothing more to press: the page changes to «Adressen er bekreftet» and
   «Takk. Nå vet vi at e-post til deg kommer fram.» «Til Myntos» goes to the
   front page.

They do not need to be signed in, and the link works in any browser.

**To get a new link:** sign in and open the front page
(`https://myntos.no/`). While the address is unconfirmed, a card there says
«Bekreft e-postadressen din. Vi sendte en lenke til …». Press «Send lenken på
nytt». The page answers «Sendt. Den forrige lenken virker ikke lenger.»: only
the newest link works.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| «Denne lenken virker ikke lenger» | The link was used already, is older than 48 hours, or a newer one was sent since. | Sign in and press «Send lenken på nytt» on the front page, then use the newest e-mail. |
| «Vi fikk ikke sendt e-posten. Prøv igjen om litt.» | The mail could not be sent just now. | Try again in a while. |
| «du har bedt om for mange e-poster den siste timen, så vent litt og prøv igjen» | Too many links in one hour. | Wait, then use the last link that arrived. |
| No card on the front page | The address is already confirmed, or this Myntos sends no e-mail at all. | Nothing to do. |
| No e-mail arrives | Spam filter, or a typo in the address at signup. | Check the spam folder. The address on the account is shown under their name on «Din konto» (`https://myntos.no/konto/`). |

## Good to know

- **Already confirmed for them:** an account made with Google or Microsoft,
  when the provider vouched for the address, and any account whose password
  was reset with a mailed link (opening that link proves the same thing).
- **Nothing is locked while unconfirmed.** Keeping books, inviting and
  accepting invitations all work as before.
- No page lets a person change the address on the account; see
  [Change your name or e-mail address](https://myntos.no/llms/help/your-account/change-your-name-or-email-address.md).
