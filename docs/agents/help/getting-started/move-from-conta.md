# Move from Conta to Myntos

In Conta the SAF-T export sits with the reports, in the section for what goes
to Skatteetaten. It comes as a ZIP and does not lock the period, so the
person must close the period themselves first; anything posted afterwards
makes the file wrong. The uploaded bilag are downloaded separately.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan flytter jeg fra Conta til Myntos?»
- «Hvor er SAF-T-eksporten i Conta?»
- "How do I move from Conta to Myntos?"

## Before they start

Read the general order first:
[Move your books to Myntos](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/move-your-books-to-myntos.md).
The guide is at `https://myntos.no/kom-i-gang/conta/`, headed «Flytte fra
Conta til Myntos».

## Steps

In Conta, as the guide «Hent dataene ut av Conta» describes:

1. **«Gå til regnskapsrapportene».** «Regnskapsrapporter → Til Skatteetaten →
   Eksporter regnskapsdata (SAF-T)».
2. **«Velg perioden du vil ha ut».** Whole financial years rather than
   months.
3. **«Last ned ZIP-en og la perioden stå i fred».** Close the period first,
   export afterwards.
4. **«Last ned bilag og vedlegg».** Uploaded bilag are a separate download.

Then in Myntos: create the account, set up the books, enter the opening
balance and register payments from the changeover date, as in the general
article.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The ZIP unpacked itself in Safari | Safari opens "safe" files after downloading. | Turn off «Åpne sikre filer etter nedlasting» under Safari's Innstillinger → Generelt and download again, so the file is kept as delivered. |
| Payroll started this year in Conta | A-meldinger are cumulative. | Finish the payroll year and send the a-melding from Conta before moving. |
| The menu is not where the guide says | Moved since the guide was checked on 13 August 2026. | Use the guide's «Kilder», Conta's own help page. |

## Good to know

- **What to keep** (the guide's «Ta vare på dette»): the SAF-T export (ZIP)
  per financial year; uploaded bilag and attachments; invoices sent from
  Conta, as PDF.
- **Myntos brings the books over from the SAF-T files, earlier years too.** On
  «Hent regnskapet fra et annet program», upload one file per year, as far
  back as they want the books in Myntos, and the running year's file up to
  today. Myntos reads them on its server (the page can be closed meanwhile),
  shows what will be posted, and posts the opening balance from the earliest
  file and every year's vouchers, under Conta's own voucher numbers. In a
  parallel run, a fresh file later shows whether the two agree:
  [Bring your books over from the previous program's SAF-T files](https://myntos.no/llms/help/opening-balance-and-year-end/bring-your-books-over-from-saf-t-files.md).
  The documents behind the vouchers (the PDFs) are not in SAF-T and stay in
  Conta's archive.
