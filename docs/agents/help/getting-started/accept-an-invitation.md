# Accept an invitation to someone's books

Somebody with access to a company's books (an owner, an accountant, a
colleague) can invite a person by e-mail. The link in that e-mail opens a page
that says who invited them, to which company, and in what role. Accepting
needs a Myntos account **with exactly the address the invitation was sent
to**; with any other address it is refused. What it gives is that one
company's books, nothing else of the sender's.

## They might ask

- «Jeg har fått en invitasjon til Myntos, hva gjør jeg?»
- «Hvordan godtar jeg invitasjonen?»
- «Invitasjonslenken virker ikke»
- "I was invited to Myntos, how do I accept?"

## Steps

1. Press the link in the invitation e-mail. The page
   (`https://myntos.no/invitasjon/?token=…`) shows «Henter invitasjonen …» and
   then a heading such as «Kari Nordmann har invitert deg til å føre
   regnskapet for Eksempel AS», with:
   - «Selskap»: the company whose books it is.
   - «Rollen din»: what they may do (see Good to know).
   - «Til»: the name the sender wrote, when they wrote one.
   - «Sendt til»: the address the invitation is for.
   - «Gjelder til»: the last day it can be used.
2. **No Myntos account yet:** press «Opprett konto». The sign-up form opens
   with the invited address already filled in and the hint «Invitasjonen
   gjelder denne adressen.» (and the name, if the sender wrote one). Keep that
   address, choose a password, and press «Opprett konto». Signing up with
   Google or Microsoft from there works too, if that provider's address is
   the invited one. See
   [Create a Myntos account](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/create-an-account.md).
3. **Already has an account with that address:** press «Jeg har konto» and
   sign in.
4. Either way they come back to the invitation automatically, which shows
   «Gir deg tilgang til regnskapet …», then «Du har nå tilgang til …», and
   sends them to the overview.

If they are already signed in with the invited address when they open the
link, step 4 happens at once.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| «Du er logget inn som … Invitasjonen gjelder …, så logg ut og inn igjen med den adressen.» | They are signed in with a different address. | Press «Logg ut» at the top right, open the link again, and use the invited address. If they have no account with that address, ask the sender to invite the address they do use. |
| «invitasjonen er sendt til en annen e-postadresse enn den du er logget inn med» | Same, caught when accepting. | As above. |
| «Denne lenken virker ikke lenger» with «Invitasjonen er brukt opp, trukket tilbake eller utløpt. Be den som inviterte deg om å sende en ny.» | Used already, withdrawn, or past «Gjelder til». | Ask the sender to send a new invitation. |
| «Lenken er ufullstendig» | The link was cut off, often by copying only part of it. | Open the link straight from the e-mail, or ask for a new one. |
| «Vi fikk ikke kontakt med Myntos akkurat nå. Prøv igjen om litt.» | No connection. | Try again later. |
| «Invitasjonen kunne ikke brukes. Be avsenderen sende en ny.» | Accepting failed for another reason. | Ask the sender for a new invitation. |

## Good to know

- **The roles**, as the page writes them:
  - «Leser: du ser regnskapet, men kan ikke endre noe»
  - «Medlem: du kan føre bilag og lage fakturaer»
  - «Administrator: du kan også styre selskaper og medlemmer»
  - «Eier: du kan alt, også abonnementet»
- **One company only.** If the sender has other companies, the person sees
  none of them, and the invitation works only for the address it was sent to,
  so a forwarded link is useless to anyone else.
- **Invitations last seven days.** After «Gjelder til», the sender has to
  invite again.
- **Their account is their own.** Accepting does not join the sender's
  account. The person can still set up books for their own company:
  [Set up the books for your company](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/set-up-the-books-for-your-company.md).
- **More than one company?** The company name at the top right of every page
  is the switcher.
- **Signing up with BankID from an invitation:** on «Fullfør registreringen»
  the address is not filled in. Type the invited address exactly.
- Nobody should be asked to share their password to "get in faster". Access
  is given by invitation, to the person's own account.
