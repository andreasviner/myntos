# After setting up the books, what to do next

When «Sett opp regnskapet» finishes, the books are ready to post in. The last
step of the page offers the one thing most people want next, letting
somebody else in, and then a few tasks decide whether the books are right
from day one. The most important of them, for a company that kept books
elsewhere before, is the opening balance. Without it the bank account,
customers and equity all start from zero.

## They might ask

- «Jeg har satt opp regnskapet, hva gjør jeg nå?»
- «Hvordan inviterer jeg regnskapsføreren min?»
- «Hvor fører jeg den første betalingen?»
- "I've set up my company in Myntos, what's next?"

## Steps

On the «Ferdig» step («Regnskapet for … er satt opp»):

1. **Invite colleagues, if anyone else should be in the books.** Under
   «Inviter kollegene dine», fill in «E-postadresse», pick a «Rolle» and press
   «Send invitasjon». The roles are:
   - «Leser: ser regnskapet, kan ikke endre noe»
   - «Medlem: kan føre bilag og lage fakturaer» (preselected)
   - «Administrator: kan også styre selskaper og medlemmer»
   - «Eier: kan alt, også abonnementet»

   Each invitation gets a line: «E-post sendt. Kommer den ikke fram, send
   lenken selv:» or «Vi fikk ikke sendt e-posten. Send lenken selv:», with the
   link and «Kopier» under it either way. The form empties, ready for the next
   address. The invitation works only for the address typed, for seven days.
2. Press «Åpne regnskapet» to go to the overview, or «Til organisasjonen» to
   the company's settings page.

Then, in roughly this order:

3. **Enter the opening balance**, if the company kept books before. The
   overview links to it at the bottom («Inngående balanse»):
   [Enter an opening balance](https://myntos.no/llms/help/opening-balance-and-year-end/enter-an-opening-balance.md).
4. **Add the bank account number**, if it was left empty. No invoice can be
   issued without it:
   [Add a bank account](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/add-a-bank-account.md).
5. **Confirm the company**, if the overview shows «ubekreftet» beside the
   name (it does when the number was typed rather than picked from Altinn):
   [Confirm the company](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/confirm-the-company.md).
6. **Register the first payment**: «Registrer betaling» on the overview, or
   «Betaling» in the top menu.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| «Vi stoppet en forespørsel som gjaldt et annet selskap enn det du ser på. Last siden på nytt.» after «Send invitasjon» | The invitation form on this step can fail this way. Nothing was sent. | Invite from the company's settings page instead: press «Til organisasjonen», then «Inviter en kollega» under «Tilgang». See [Invite a colleague](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/invite-a-colleague.md). |
| «denne e-postadressen har allerede tilgang til selskapet» | That person is already in these books. | Nothing to do. |
| The person says no e-mail came | Myntos sees only that the mail service took the message, not that it arrived. | Press «Kopier» on that line and send them the link yourself. Once the page is left, use «Send på nytt» on the company's settings page: [Send an invitation again](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/send-an-invitation-again.md). |
| «Vi fikk ikke sendt e-posten. Send lenken selv:» | The invitation exists, but the mail did not go out. | Press «Kopier» and send the link to that person yourself. It works only for the address typed. |

## Good to know

- **An invitation lets a person into these books only**, not into the
  person's other companies, and only with the address it was sent to.
- **Who can do what** is decided by the role, and can be changed later on the
  settings page:
  [Roles and permissions](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/roles-and-permissions.md).
- **An AI as a helper.** To let an AI agent work in the books, the person
  makes it a key of its own. Never give an AI the password:
  [Invite an AI](https://myntos.no/llms/help/ai/invite-an-ai.md).
- **History stays where it is.** Entries from before the first financial year
  in Myntos are not posted again; they stay in the old program's SAF-T file
  and archive:
  [Move your books to Myntos](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/move-your-books-to-myntos.md).
