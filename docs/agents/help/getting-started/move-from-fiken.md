# Move from Fiken to Myntos

Fiken keeps every export on one page and gives both the SAF-T file and a
complete copy of the bilag archive. Take both: SAF-T is the figures, the
archive is the documentation behind them. Download everything **before**
cancelling Fiken, because access to the export page ends with the
subscription while the duty to keep the books does not.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan flytter jeg regnskapet fra Fiken til Myntos?»
- «Hva må jeg laste ned fra Fiken før jeg sier opp?»
- "How do I move from Fiken to Myntos?"

## Before they start

Read the general order first (move at a period break, finish payroll, close
the period, then export):
[Move your books to Myntos](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/move-your-books-to-myntos.md).
The guide itself is at `https://myntos.no/kom-i-gang/fiken/`, headed «Flytte
fra Fiken til Myntos».

## Steps

In Fiken, as the guide «Hent dataene ut av Fiken» describes:

1. **«Åpne eksportsiden».** All exports are together under «Foretak →
   Eksport av regnskap». Nothing has to be closed to download, and exports
   can be run as often as they like.
2. **«Last ned SAF-T-eksporten».** Fiken makes one SAF-T file per financial
   year. Download one for every year kept in Fiken, not only the last.
3. **«Last ned komplett eksport».** A ZIP with the SAF-T file and Excel
   overviews for every year, and every uploaded document, invoice, order,
   quote and credit note. Myntos reads it as it is: uploaded on «Hent
   regnskapet fra et annet program», the SAF-T files are taken out of it, and
   when the books are posted Myntos keeps every document in it with the
   folder it was in. They are not put on the vouchers, but are found and
   downloaded under «Dokumenter fra eksporten» on the same page:
   [Bring your books over from the previous program's SAF-T files](https://myntos.no/llms/help/opening-balance-and-year-end/bring-your-books-over-from-saf-t-files.md),
   [Find the documents from Fiken's complete export](https://myntos.no/llms/help/opening-balance-and-year-end/find-the-documents-from-fikens-export.md).
4. **«Har du kjørt lønn, ta lønnsslippene også».** Payslips are exported
   separately and are not in SAF-T Regnskap.

Then in Myntos: create the account, set up the books, enter the opening
balance and register payments from the changeover date, as in the general
article's steps 6 to 9.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The menu path is not where the guide says | Fiken moved it. The guide was checked on 13 August 2026. | Use the sources the guide lists under «Kilder»: Fiken's own help pages «Eksport av regnskap fra Fiken» and «Inngående balanse med SAF-T-fil». |
| Payroll is half done for the year | A-meldinger are cumulative. | Finish the payroll year and send the a-meldinger from Fiken before moving. |

## Good to know

- **What to keep** (the guide's «Ta vare på dette»): the SAF-T file for each
  financial year kept in Fiken; the complete export (ZIP) with bilag,
  invoices and Excel overviews; payslips, if they ran payroll.
- **Starting on 1 January**, the SAF-T file up to and including 31 December
  the year before is the starting point.
- **Myntos brings the books over from the SAF-T files, earlier years too.** On
  «Hent regnskapet fra et annet program», upload one file per year, as far
  back as they want the books in Myntos, and the running year's file up to
  today. Myntos reads them on its server (the page can be closed meanwhile),
  shows what will be posted, and posts the opening balance from the earliest
  file and every year's vouchers, under Fiken's own voucher numbers. In a
  parallel run, a fresh file later shows whether the two agree:
  [Bring your books over from the previous program's SAF-T files](https://myntos.no/llms/help/opening-balance-and-year-end/bring-your-books-over-from-saf-t-files.md).
  The documents behind the vouchers (the PDFs) are not in SAF-T and stay in
  Fiken's archive.
