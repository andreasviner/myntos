# Find what is behind a figure in the accounts

Every line of «Resultatregnskap» and «Balanse» that sums accounts can be
opened to show them, and every account's amount leads to that account's
vouchers for the year.

## They might ask

- «Hvorfor er andre driftskostnader så høye i fjor?»
- «Hvilke kontoer ligger under varekostnad?»
- «Hvor ser jeg bilagene bak et tall i regnskapet?»
- "How do I drill down into a line of the accounts in Myntos?"

## Before they start

Anyone with access to the company can do this, whatever their role.

## Steps

1. Open «Regnskapet år for år» from the overview
   (`https://myntos.no/regnskap/`).
2. Press the name of a line with a small arrow in front of it, such as
   «Andre driftskostnader». The accounts under it appear beneath, each with
   its number and name and its amount per year. Press the name again to fold
   them away.
3. Press an account's amount in a year. «Bilag» opens on that account and
   year: every voucher on the account, with the balance after each.

The lines without an arrow («Sum driftsinntekter», «Driftsresultat»,
«Årsresultat», «Udisponert resultat» and the other sums) add up the lines above
them and have no accounts of their own.

## Good to know

- An amount shown as 0 in a year has no link: there is nothing behind it.
- A voucher cannot be changed or deleted. A mistake is corrected where the
  voucher was made: a payment under «Betaling», a manual voucher under «Manuelt
  bilag», and a voucher from the previous program in that program.
