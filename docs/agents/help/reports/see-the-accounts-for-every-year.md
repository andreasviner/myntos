# See the accounts for every year

The overview shows one year, the current one. «Regnskapet år for år» shows
every year the company has in Myntos, side by side: the result
(«Resultatregnskap») and what the company owns and owes («Balanse»). Years
brought over from the previous program are there too. It only shows; nothing
on the page changes the books.

## They might ask

- «Hvor ser jeg regnskapet for i fjor?»
- «Jeg hentet 2025 fra Tripletex, men forsiden viser bare null. Hvor er det?»
- «Hva var resultatet i fjor?»
- «Kan jeg sammenligne årene?»
- "Where do I see previous years in Myntos?"

## Before they start

Anyone with access to the company can see it, whatever their role.

## Steps

1. On the overview (`https://myntos.no/`), press «Regnskapet år for år» at the
   top right, beside «Registrer betaling». It is also linked in the note at the
   bottom of the overview, and from the card «Ingenting er ført i … ennå» when
   the current year is empty. The page is `https://myntos.no/regnskap/`.
2. Each year is a column, newest first. Under each year it says what state the
   year is in: «avsluttet» (closed), «ikke avsluttet», «ført til …» (the date
   of the last voucher in a year still running), or «ingen bilag» (nothing
   posted in it yet).
3. «Resultatregnskap»: «Salgsinntekter», «Andre driftsinntekter», «Sum
   driftsinntekter», «Varekostnad», «Lønnskostnad», «Avskrivninger», «Andre
   driftskostnader», «Sum driftskostnader», «Driftsresultat», the financial
   lines, «Resultat før skattekostnad», «Skattekostnad» and «Årsresultat».
   Lines with nothing on them in any year are left out.
4. «Balanse»: what the company owns («Sum eiendeler», with «Bankinnskudd og
   kontanter» among them) and what it owes and its equity («Sum egenkapital og
   gjeld»). The two sums are always equal.
5. To see what is behind a figure, see
   [Find what is behind a figure](https://myntos.no/llms/help/reports/find-what-is-behind-a-figure.md).
6. With more than four years, «Vis alle … årene» at the top shows the rest.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| «Ingenting er ført ennå» | No voucher is posted in any year | Register payments, or bring the books over: [Bring your books over from SAF-T files](https://myntos.no/llms/help/opening-balance-and-year-end/bring-your-books-over-from-saf-t-files.md) |
| A year reads «ingen bilag» and all zeros | Nothing is posted in that year yet, often the current year after importing only last year | Import that year from the old program as well, or register its payments and import the bank statements |
| «Udisponert resultat» under equity | The year (or an earlier one) is not closed, so its result has not been moved to equity yet | Normal for a year still running; a year that is over is closed under «Årsavslutning»: [Close a financial year](https://myntos.no/llms/help/opening-balance-and-year-end/close-a-financial-year.md) |
| «Skattekostnad» missing for a year that is over | The tax is entered when the year is closed | Close the year under «Årsavslutning» |

## Good to know

- The figures are read off what is posted, without VAT. They are not a filed
  årsregnskap or skattemelding.
- A reversed voucher and its reversal cancel out, so a corrected payment counts
  once.
- The overview's «Inn i år» and «Ut i år» are money through the bank this
  year, which is not the same as income and costs: an invoice not yet paid is
  income here but not money in.
