# Change or set your password

«Din konto» shows whether the account has a password («Passord»: «Satt» or
«Ikke satt») but has **no form for changing it**. Both changing a password and
setting a first one on an account made with Google or Microsoft go through the
mailed link from «Glemt passordet?», even while signed in. The link signs out
every device and stops every key the person made for an AI agent, which is
what someone changing a password because they fear it is known wants anyway.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan bytter jeg passord i Myntos?»
- «Hvor er knappen for å endre passord?»
- «Jeg logger inn med Google, kan jeg få et passord også?»
- "How do I change my Myntos password?"

## Before they start

- This Myntos must send e-mail.
- An account that signs in with **BankID** has no password and cannot get
  one this way; see Good to know.

## Steps

1. Open `https://myntos.no/glemt-passord/` (or, when signed out, «Glemt
   passordet?» under the form on «Logg inn»).
2. Fill in «E-postadresse» with the address on the account (shown under
   their name on «Din konto») and press «Send lenke».
3. Open the e-mail and press the link, within half an hour.
4. On «Velg nytt passord», type the new password in «Nytt passord» and press
   «Lagre passordet».
5. Every device is now signed out. Press «Logg inn» and sign in with the new
   password.

Step by step with every message:
[Reset a forgotten password](https://myntos.no/llms/help/getting-started/reset-a-forgotten-password.md).

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| «kontoen logger inn med BankID og kan ikke få passord med denne lenken. …», or an e-mail saying the account signs in with BankID | A BankID account. | Switch instead: [Switch from BankID to e-mail and password](https://myntos.no/llms/help/your-account/switch-from-bankid-to-email.md). |
| «Denne installasjonen av Myntos sender ikke e-post, så passordet kan ikke tilbakestilles herfra. …» | No e-mail on this Myntos. | Contact whoever runs it. |

## Good to know

- **Everything is signed out**, this device included, and every key made for
  an AI agent stops. Afterwards they make the AI a new key:
  [Invite an AI](https://myntos.no/llms/help/ai/invite-an-ai.md).
- **Google or Microsoft keeps working** beside a newly set password.
- **The hint on «Din konto»** («… logg den ut og bytt passord») means this
  flow; there is no other.
- Nobody, an AI included, should be told the new password or given the link.
