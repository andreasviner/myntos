# Change your name or e-mail address

«Din konto» shows the person's name as its heading and the account's e-mail
address under it. **No page in Myntos lets a person change either today.**
The name matters more than it looks: it is what the books show beside every
entry the person registers, and the books keep it.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan endrer jeg navnet mitt i Myntos?»
- «Navnet mitt er stavet feil»
- «Kan jeg bytte e-postadresse på kontoen?»
- "How do I change the email address on my Myntos account?"

## Steps

1. To see what is stored: open the menu at the top right and press «Din
   konto» (`https://myntos.no/konto/`). The name is the heading; the address
   is the line under it.
2. To have either corrected, write to kontakt@myntos.no from the address on
   the account, saying what is wrong and what it should be. The privacy
   statement («personvernerklæringen») gives everyone the right to have
   wrong information corrected, and names that address for it.

## Good to know

- **Where the name came from:** what they typed at signup, or what Google,
  Microsoft or BankID gave.
- **Do not make a second account to get a new address.** A new account
  starts with nothing: access to books is per account, so it would need
  every company's owner to invite it again, and the entries made by the old
  account stay in the old account's name.
- **An invitation is tied to an address.** If someone invited the new
  address, the person needs an account with that address to accept it, or
  should ask for the invitation to go to the address their account has.
