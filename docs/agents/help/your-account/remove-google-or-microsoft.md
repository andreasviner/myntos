# Remove Google or Microsoft sign-in

A linked Google or Microsoft sign-in can be removed from «Din konto», as long
as it is not the only way into the account. Myntos never lets the last way in
go, because an account nobody can open would still hold its companies.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan fjerner jeg Google-innloggingen fra Myntos?»
- «Jeg vil ikke lenger logge inn med Microsoft»
- "How do I unlink Google from my Myntos account?"

## Before they start

The account needs another way in: a password, or the other provider. An
account made with Google has no password until one is set; see
[Change or set your password](https://myntos.no/llms/help/your-account/change-or-set-your-password.md).

## Steps

1. Open the menu at the top right and press «Din konto»
   (`https://myntos.no/konto/`).
2. In «Slik logger du inn», find the row for Google or Microsoft.
3. Press «Fjern» on that row. No confirmation is asked; it can be linked
   again at any time with «Koble til Google» or «Koble til Microsoft».
4. The card says «Innlogging med … er fjernet.»

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No «Fjern» button, and the row ends «Den eneste måten inn, så den kan ikke fjernes.» | It is the only way into the account. | Set a password first ([Change or set your password](https://myntos.no/llms/help/your-account/change-or-set-your-password.md)) or link the other provider, then remove it. |
| «dette er den eneste måten å logge inn på kontoen. Sett et passord eller koble til en annen innlogging først.» | Same, caught when pressing. | As above. |
| A BankID row with «Den eneste måten inn på kontoen.» and no button | BankID is never removed with a button. | The way out of BankID is the switch: [Switch from BankID to e-mail and password](https://myntos.no/llms/help/your-account/switch-from-bankid-to-email.md). |

## Good to know

- **Devices stay signed in.** Removing a provider does not end the sessions
  it opened. End them under «Innloggingene dine»:
  [Sign out other devices](https://myntos.no/llms/help/your-account/sign-out-other-devices.md).
