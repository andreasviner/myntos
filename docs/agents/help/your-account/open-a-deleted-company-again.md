# Open a deleted company again

A company with anything entered is not deleted at once. Its accounts are
kept locked until 31 December five years after its last financial year, as
bokføringsloven § 13 requires, and deleted for good the day after. Until
then, an owner of the company finds it under «Slettede selskaper» on their
account page and can open it again, exactly as it was. That is how to fetch a
receipt for a tax audit, or download a SAF-T file again, after leaving
Myntos.

## They might ask

- «Jeg slettet selskapet, hvordan får jeg tak i et bilag?»
- «Kan jeg angre at jeg slettet selskapet?»
- «Hvor ble det slettede selskapet av?»
- "How do I restore a company I deleted in Myntos?"

## Before they start

- They must still be an «Eier» of the company and still have their account.
  Other members lost their access when the company was deleted, and nobody
  can open it after the last owner has deleted their account.
- The date shown has not passed. After it, the accounts are gone for good.

## Steps

1. Open the menu at the top right and press «Din konto»
   (`https://myntos.no/konto/`).
2. The card «Slettede selskaper» lists each company with its organisation
   number, the day it was deleted and «oppbevares til …», the last day it is
   kept. The card only appears when there is at least one.
3. Press «Åpne igjen» beside the company. The front page opens on it.
4. To delete it again when done, follow
   [Delete a company](https://myntos.no/llms/help/company-and-access/delete-a-company.md).
   The date is worked out anew.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No «Slettede selskaper» card | They own no deleted company that is still kept: it was deleted at once (nothing entered), the date has passed, or they were not an owner. | Ask an owner of the company. |
| «det er satt opp et nytt regnskap for … etter at dette ble slettet. …» | New books for the same organisation number were set up in the account after this one was deleted. Two open sets for one number are not allowed. | Delete the new books first (they are deleted at once if nothing is entered in them), then press «Åpne igjen». |
| «et annet regnskap har bekreftet eierskapet til … etter at dette ble slettet. …» | Deleting released the company's confirmed ownership, and somebody has since confirmed the same organisation number in other books. | Write to kontakt@myntos.no, as the message says. |

## Good to know

- **Only the accounts come back.** Colleagues who lost access are invited
  again, keys for AI agents are made again, and access for AI agents is
  switched on again by whoever decides that.
- **Deleting the account** while owning a deleted company leaves the company
  kept until its date, but nobody can open it again after that. The account
  page says so beside it under «Slett kontoen».
