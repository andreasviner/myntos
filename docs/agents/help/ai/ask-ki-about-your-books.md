# Ask Myntos KI anything about your books

The **KI** tab in the top menu (`https://myntos.no/ki/`) opens a chat window
titled «Jeg har lest hele regnskapet ditt. Spør meg om hva som helst.». Every
question is answered directly from the company's live books (income statement,
balance sheet, unpaid invoices, incoming unbooked bills, deadlines, and up to
25 relevant posted vouchers looked up automatically for the question).

## They might ask

- «Kan jeg chatte med regnskapet mitt i Myntos?»
- «Hvor er KI-chat i Myntos?»
- «Lagres samtalene mine i KI-chat?»
- «Hvilke data sendes når jeg spør KI-en?»
- "How do I ask AI questions about my accounting in Myntos?"

## Before they start

- Signed in with the right company chosen at the top right.
- The role «Medlem», «Administrator» or «Eier» to send questions («Leser» can
  read saved conversations).
- KI switched on for the company under «Innstillinger for selskapet» →
  «KI i Myntos» (`https://myntos.no/organisasjon/#ki`).

## Steps

1. Click **KI** in the top menu (`https://myntos.no/ki/`).
2. Click one of the suggested questions or type your own question in the box
   («Spør om kostnader, fakturaer, bilag, mva eller frister …»).
3. Under the box, choose the level at «KI-nivå»: «Gratis» (always unlimited),
   «Medium» or «Smart» (requires a KI plan with weekly quota left).
4. Press **Send** (or Enter; Shift+Enter adds a new line). The answer streams
   directly in the conversation.
5. Use **+ Ny samtale** in the left sidebar to start a fresh thread, click any
   saved thread to continue it, or press **×** beside a thread to delete it.

## If something stops them

| What they see | Why | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| «KI er slått av for dette selskapet» | KI is switched off for this company | An owner or administrator clicks «Slå på KI i innstillinger» (`https://myntos.no/organisasjon/#ki`) and unticks «Slå av KI for …» |
| «Medium» and «Smart» are greyed out | The company has no active KI plan or this week's quota is spent | Ask with «Gratis» (unlimited), wait until Monday 00:00 when the weekly quota resets, or have the owner choose/upgrade a plan at `https://myntos.no/ki/about/#planer` |
| «KI svarte ikke akkurat nå. Prøv igjen om litt.» | Løynd did not answer | Try sending the question again in a moment |

## Good to know

- Nothing in KI chat posts or changes anything in the ledger: it only reads the
  books and answers questions.
- Saved threads belong strictly to one company, stay in Myntos's database in
  the EEA, and are deleted if you delete the thread or when the company is
  deleted.
- Løynd runs inside the EEA, keeps nothing, and trains no models on the books.
  PDFs and receipt images are not sent in KI chat, only structured figures and
  voucher text.
