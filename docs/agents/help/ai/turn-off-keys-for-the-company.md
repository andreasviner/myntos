# Turn off keys for the company

## They might ask

- «Hvordan slår jeg av all KI-tilgang til regnskapet?»
- «Kan jeg sperre alle API-nøkler?»

## Before they start

Only an «Eier» or «Administrator» can.

## Steps

1. «Innstillinger for selskapet» → «Tilgang», the card «KI-agenter og
   API-nøkler».
2. Press «Slå av nøkler for selskapet» at the bottom of the card.
3. Read the warning: every key stops at once, colleagues' too, and they cannot
   be switched on again; what they were waiting to have approved is never
   carried out.
4. Press «Slå av».

## Good to know

- Switching keys on again later starts from nothing: every agent needs a new
  key. That is deliberate, so a forgotten key cannot wake up.
- This is separate from Myntos's own KI (reading receipts), which is switched
  off on the KI page: https://myntos.no/llms/help/ai/turn-myntos-ki-on-or-off.md.
