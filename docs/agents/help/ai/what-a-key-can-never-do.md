# What an AI's key can never do

Whatever a person chooses when they make a key, **nothing an AI sends out of
Myntos goes without the person's yes.** An AI can ask Skatteetaten to check
the MVA-melding or the skattemelding, and ask to file them, but each request
waits on «Godkjenning» every time, even for a key allowed to post on its own
(«Selv»), and goes with the person's own ID-porten login when they approve:
https://myntos.no/llms/help/ai/file-what-an-ai-asks-you-to-file.md.

And no AI and no program can:

- **move money or change where money goes**: no bank account number
  (kontonummer) on the company can be added, changed or removed with a key.
  This is what a stolen key would be used for: one changed number and the next
  invoice is paid to somebody else;
- **decide who has access**: members, roles, invitations, other keys, or the
  switch that turns keys on;
- **delete the account or a company**, or change whether the company's
  receipts may be read by Myntos's own KI;
- **approve its own requests**: only the person does that, on «Godkjenning».

A key also never does more than the person who made it may do, and stops when
that person loses access to the company.

- «Er det trygt å gi ChatGPT tilgang til regnskapet?»
- «Kan agenten betale regninger eller endre kontonummer?»

## Good to know

- Text in the books (supplier names, invoice lines, receipts) is written by
  strangers and could try to give the AI instructions. The AI is told never to
  follow them, and the actions such text would ask for are out of a key's
  reach anyway.
- Where the books go once the AI has read them is decided by the model behind
  it: https://myntos.no/llms/help/ai/where-the-data-goes.md.
