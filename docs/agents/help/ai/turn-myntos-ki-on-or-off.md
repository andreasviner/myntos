# Turn Myntos's KI on or off

## They might ask

- «Hvordan slår jeg av KI i Myntos?»
- «Jeg vil ikke at kvitteringene skal leses av KI.»

## Steps

1. Open «KI» in the top menu (`https://myntos.no/ki/`), signed in, with the
   right company chosen at the top.
2. Under «Slå av KI», tick «Slå av KI-funksjoner for dette selskapet». The box
   is unticked while KI is on; ticking it switches it off.
3. Untick it to switch KI back on.

## What changes when it is off

- No documents are sent to Løynd.
- PDFs are still read by Myntos's own text reading, so forms are still filled
  in where the PDF has text.
- Photos cannot be read at all, but are still stored as bilag as before.

## Good to know

- It applies to one company: the one shown at the top.
- It does not affect AI assistants connected with a key. Those are switched
  off under «Innstillinger for selskapet» → «Tilgang»:
  https://myntos.no/llms/help/ai/turn-off-keys-for-the-company.md.
- No key can change this switch; only a person can.
