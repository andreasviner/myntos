# Decline what an AI asks to do

A declined request is never carried out. The person can say why, and the AI
can read the reason and do better next time.

## They might ask

- «Hvordan avviser jeg det agenten ba om?»
- «Agenten har bedt om noe feil. Hvordan stopper jeg det?»

## Steps

1. Open «Godkjenning» (`https://myntos.no/godkjenning/`).
2. On the card under «Venter på deg», press «Avvis».
3. Optionally write why in the box («Hvorfor? (valgfritt, agenten får lese
   det)»), for example «Dette er privat, ikke firmaets».
4. Press «Avvis forespørselen». It moves to «Avgjort» as «Avvist», with the
   reason.

## Good to know

- The person who made the key can decline its requests, and so can an owner or
  administrator, the same people who may stop a colleague's key.
- Declining one request does not stop the key. To stop the key, see
  https://myntos.no/llms/help/ai/see-and-stop-keys.md.
- The AI can also withdraw its own request; it then shows as «Trukket
  tilbake av agenten».
